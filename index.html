"What even is Copilot?"

Copilot is Microsoft's built-in AI assistant. Think of it as a knowledgeable colleague you can type a request to — it helps you draft documents, summarise information, create plans, research topics, prepare for meetings, and much more.

The good news? If your organisation uses Microsoft 365, you most likely already have access to a free version of Copilot right now. You just might not know it's there — or how to make it work for you.

No jargon. No tech background needed. My job is to make this immediately useful for your team — not to overwhelm you with buzzwords about artificial intelligence.
